FAQ

Questions, answered.

What platforms does SLeads monitor? +
SLeads is designed around Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn as its lead discovery sources.
How does AI scoring work? +
Conversations can be evaluated against your business description, keywords and buyer-intent criteria to produce a relevance score from 0 to 100.
Can I save leads? +
Yes. The dashboard includes a Save Lead workflow so qualified opportunities can be organized for later.
Do I get access immediately after payment? +
The intended account flow is payment verification, signup, login and then administrator approval before dashboard access is activated.
Can I upgrade my plan? +
Yes. Your account is linked to its active subscription plan and the dashboard can reflect the updated limits.
